# Scratch training result — 3 November 2026

Completed 12 epochs of real fine-tuning on a free Colab Tesla T4 using 800 training photos, 160 validation photos and 160 reserved test photos. The script pins dataset and baseline revisions, remaps classes, excludes images without label files and removes exact cross-split duplicates.

| Held-out scratch metric | Original | Trained candidate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Precision at Ultralytics selected F1 point | 15.9% | 24.9% |
| Recall at that point | 19.3% | 21.2% |
| AP50 | 6.6% | 14.1% |
| AP50–95 | 2.0% | 5.4% |
| Small scratch matches, confidence .45 / IoU .5 | 3 / 99 | 0 / 99 |

**Decision: do not deploy this candidate.** General scratch metrics improved on this dataset, but small-scratch performance worsened. Absolute scores remain inadequate, label audit found overly broad damage boxes, and the VehiDE evaluation subset lacks some damage classes. The pilot does not justify a best-model or hairline-scratch accuracy claim. The existing live detector is unchanged.

Small means an annotation box covering at most 1% of the image, not a scratch width in millimetres. Labels include automatically generated annotations and do not establish an independent vehicle-level benchmark. Better tiny-scratch training needs a manually checked collection of sharp close-ups and intact panels under varied lighting, with an independent test set.

The live backend now preserves new uploads at up to 3072 pixels, JPEG quality 95 and 4:4:4 chroma while stripping EXIF. All 22 application tests and two training-data regression checks passed. This improves the retained input detail; it does not prove scratch accuracy.
